## Anomalous Morphology

![](/docs/examples/15.png)

This "anomalous morphology" checkbox is to mark anything strange that cannot be well classified within the other checkboxes. For example, in this case that large flux increase that can be seen is very unusual. In the middle of the transit the flux returns almost to normal value. Almost certainly that is a flare from the star in the middle of the transit. In any case, "Interior flux increase" was also marked. What other things could cause unusual behavior? Well, for example a transit of a planet with rings, or two transits overlapping at the same time...
